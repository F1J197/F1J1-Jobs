# VitiWork, not FijiJobs

Shipping as FijiJobs collides with [fijijobs.com](https://fijijobs.com), a competitor the vault already judged a weak UX. The vault chose **VitiWork** ("Find work in Fiji"). The prototype in this repo still says FijiJobs — that is a rename, not a new product.

**Considered options:** keep FijiJobs for continuity with this repo's current UI; pick a third name. Continuity loses to the collision. A third name throws away first-party brand work in `vitiwork-landing` and the vault.
