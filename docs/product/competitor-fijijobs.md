# FijiJobs.com

Primary competitor in Fiji's online job market. Direct target for disruption by VitiWork.

**Do not ship as FijiJobs.** [fijijobs.com](https://fijijobs.com) already occupies that name.

## Assessment

### Strengths

- Established brand recognition
- Basic functionality works

### Weaknesses (Disruption Opportunities)

1. **Horrible UX** — Andrew's direct experience as expat job seeker
2. **No mobile optimization** — Critical in Fiji where mobile is primary
3. **No skills matching** — Manual browsing only
4. **No diaspora connection** — Missing expat/Fiji-born overseas talent pool
5. **Outdated design** — Looks like early-2000s job board

## Strategic Implications

The gap between FijiJobs.com's offering and first-world job platform standards is enormous. This validates the VitiWork opportunity: bring modern UX, mobile-first design, skills-based matching, and diaspora connectivity to Fiji.
