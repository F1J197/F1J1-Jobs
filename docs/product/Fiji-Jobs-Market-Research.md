## Market Fundamentals

- Labour force: ~353,100 (2017 est.)
- Unemployment: 5.3-5.7%
- Workforce: Agriculture 44.2% | Services 41.6% | Industry 14.3%
- Youth unemployment: Data trapped in PDFs

## Gaps & Opportunities (10x Better)

1. **Mobile-first, lightweight** — 126% mobile penetration vs desktop incumbents
2. **WhatsApp/SMS apply** — skip app downloads
3. **Simplicity & speed** — 1-tap apply vs MyJobsFiji bloat
4. **Hyper-local casual/gig** — hospitality, retail, agriculture unaddressed
5. **SME-friendly pricing** — FJD $15/post vs $100
6. **Instant notifications** — location-based push/SMS alerts
7. **Government partnership** — no central job board exists
