# VitiWork Product Specification

**Tagline:** Find Work in Fiji  
**Priority:** #1 (attack first)  
**MVP Timeline:** 48 hours to first prototype

---

## Why Jobs First?

- Faster validation cycle than M&A
- Higher frequency (people job-hunt more than buy businesses)
- Network effects kick in faster
- Lower trust barrier
- Employment data becomes moat for future verticals

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** Job seekers 18-45, mobile-first, casual/gig workers
- **Secondary:** SME employers in hospitality, retail, agriculture, construction

## Core MVP Features

1. Mobile-first job feed (card-based, 360px)
2. Simple category filters (Hospitality, Retail, Construction, Agriculture, Drivers, Professional)
3. WhatsApp "Express Interest" button (no login required)
4. Employer post-a-job form (FJD $15-25/post, or free first 10)
5. SMS/WhatsApp notification for matching jobs
6. Basic employer verification (manual for launch)

## Key Differentiators vs MyJobsFiji

| Dimension | MyJobsFiji | VitiWork |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Pricing | FJD $100/post | FJD $15/post |
| Mobile UX | Desktop-table | Mobile-first native |
| Apply method | Form/email | WhatsApp 1-tap |
| Focus | White-collar | Casual/gig/trade |
| Notifications | None | SMS/WhatsApp alerts |
| Categories | Generic | Fiji-specific |

## Business Rules

- Free: 1 post, 7 days
- Standard: FJD $15, 30 days
- Featured: FJD $25, 30 days, top feed
- Subscription: FJD $49/month, unlimited
- Auto-expire after duration
- Renewal reminder 3 days before via WhatsApp

## Validation Questions

1. Would you use this instead of Facebook groups?
2. Is WhatsApp apply better than filling out a form?
3. What pay rate would make you apply immediately?
4. Would you tell your boss about this for hiring?
