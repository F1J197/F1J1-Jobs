## Key Implications for Product Design

1. **Android-first, 360px design** — 81% of users, smaller screens
2. **Facebook is the internet** — integrate sharing, consider login
3. **WhatsApp = identity** — no mandatory email signup
4. **Bandwidth-conscious** — <500KB initial load
5. **Mobile money ready** — M-PAiSA/MyCash integration
6. **Young population** — median 27.2, mobile-native
