# eJobsFiji — competitor facts

Primary source: [ejobsfiji.com](https://ejobsfiji.com/) retrieved 2026-08-19.

## Who they are

eJobsFiji brands itself "Fiji's No.1 Job Board." The homepage offers category browse (Academic through Trades & Services), claims 500+ active jobs, 100+ companies, 10k+ Seekers, and nationwide Fiji coverage.

## Android-first

A homepage block promotes the eJobsFiji Android app on Google Play: search, alerts, and saved Listings from the phone. Oldest visible career posts on the site date to January–February 2022, which matches a 2022 local-board start.

## Live Listing mix (homepage sample)

Hospitality (Tokatoka, Palms Denarau, Tokoroki), banking/funds (Apex Fund Services, Pepper Advantage), and mixed office roles — not only casual work. Towns in the sample: Suva, Nadi, Denarau.

## Design implication for VitiWork

eJobsFiji is a real local board, not a rumour. VitiWork still wins on no-account Express Interest and SME pricing; it does not win by pretending no Android-first competitor exists.
