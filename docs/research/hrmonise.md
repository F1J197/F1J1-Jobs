# HRmonise — competitor facts

Primary source: [jobs.hrmonise.com](https://jobs.hrmonise.com/) retrieved 2026-08-19.

## Who they are

The board lists corporate and recruitment-agency roles (Centrecom, Maxumise Recruitment, Fijian Broadcasting Corporation, Fiji Sun, CEVA, Nauru Utilities). Closings are date-driven. Salary is often "N/A". Mix is full-time professional and some part-time media — not a consumer Seek feed.

## What it is not

This is an HCM / ATS careers portal, not a Seeker-first marketplace. Several listings are "EOI" or talent-pool ads. Some roles are outside Fiji (Nauru).

## Design implication for VitiWork

Do not model VitiWork on HRmonise. Corporate HCM can remain a later Employer export; the Seeker path is ListingCatalog + Express Interest.
