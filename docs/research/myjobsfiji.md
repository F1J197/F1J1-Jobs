# MyJobsFiji — competitor facts

Primary sources only. Retrieved 2026-08-19.

## Who they are

MyJobsFiji presents itself as Fiji's job board since 2018. The about page claims 80,000+ registered candidates, 25,000+ roles placed, and coverage from Suva to Savusavu. It is "not a registered recruitment agency" — advertising only; hiring decisions stay with the Employer.

Source: [About us](https://myjobsfiji.com/about/)

## Pricing (FJD, prepaid)

From [Employer products / Pricing](https://myjobsfiji.com/employer-products/):

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single Post | FJD 100 | 1 advertisement, 28 days |
| Four Pack | FJD 300 | 4 advertisements, 31 days each, use over 365 days (~FJD 75/post) |
| Enterprise | FJD 2,000 | Unlimited advertisements, 45 days each, 12 months |

Included on listed plans: colour logo/profile, no word limit, featured logo rotation, dashboard, website redirect, candidate alert emails, automatic Facebook listing, social "special mention" posts.

## Apply wall and eligibility

Seekers register, upload a CV, and apply on-platform. Terms (last updated June 2026):

- Eligible Seekers are **residents of Fiji who hold the right to work** in the country.
- Eligible Employers hire in Fiji, or hire Fiji residents overseas.
- Payment is prepaid in FJD before a Listing goes live.
- Unused plans can be refunded; used plans become account credit.

Source: [Terms of use](https://myjobsfiji.com/terms-of-use/)

## Design implication for VitiWork

A Free First Post plus WhatsApp Express Interest without an account is the contrast: MyJobsFiji charges FJD 100 and requires an account to apply.
