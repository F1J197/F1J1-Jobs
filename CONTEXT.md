# VitiWork

A job board for people in Fiji looking for work, and for Employers in Fiji who are hiring. Terms here are the glossary — not a spec.

## People

**Seeker**:
A person in Fiji looking for work in any Category.
_Avoid_: Candidate, applicant, job seeker, talent

**Employer**:
A person or company hiring in Fiji.
_Avoid_: Recruiter, company (when you mean the hiring actor)

## Work

**Listing**:
A published job with Town, Category, type, pay, and expiry.
_Avoid_: Job post, vacancy, advert, role (when you mean the published record)

**Category**:
The kind of work on a Listing — Hospitality, Retail, Construction, Agriculture, Drivers, Trades, Professional, or Government.
_Avoid_: Industry (when you mean our Category list)

**Town**:
A Fiji place name on a Listing (Suva, Nadi, Lautoka, Labasa, …). Not a travel guide.
_Avoid_: Location, island, area (when you mean the Listing field)

## Contact

**Express Interest**:
A Seeker contacts an Employer via WhatsApp without an account.
_Avoid_: Apply, one-click apply, WhatsApp apply (when you mean this path)

**Application**:
A Seeker submits through a VitiWork profile. Optional path; not the default.
_Avoid_: Interest, apply (when you mean the profile path)

## Employer publishing

**Free First Post**:
An Employer's first Listing is free and lasts seven days.
_Avoid_: Free trial, promo post

**Standard**:
A paid Listing that lasts thirty days.
_Avoid_: Basic

**Featured**:
A paid Listing that lasts thirty days and sits at the top of the feed.
_Avoid_: Premium, sponsored (when you mean this tier)

**Verification**:
A manual Employer check at launch.
_Avoid_: KYC, badge (when you mean this check)
