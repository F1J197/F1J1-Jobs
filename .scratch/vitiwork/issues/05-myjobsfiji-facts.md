# 05 — MyJobsFiji facts

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

What does MyJobsFiji actually charge, who may apply, and how long has it been the incumbent? Cite the first-party research note, not rumour.

## Answer

Cited from `docs/research/myjobsfiji.md` (retrieved 2026-08-19 from myjobsfiji.com):

- Single Post **FJD 100 / 28 days** (Four Pack FJD 300; Enterprise FJD 2,000).
- Seekers register, upload a CV, and **need an account to apply**.
- Eligible Seekers are **Fiji residents with the right to work**.
- Board since **2018**; about page claims **80,000+ registered candidates**.

VitiWork’s contrast is a Free First Post plus WhatsApp Express Interest with no account.
