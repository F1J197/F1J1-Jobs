## Question

Is HRmonise a consumer Seek-style board we should copy, or something else?

## Answer

Cited from `docs/research/hrmonise.md` (retrieved 2026-08-19 from jobs.hrmonise.com):

- **Corporate HCM / ATS careers portal**, not a consumer Seek.
- Mix is full-time professional and some part-time media; several listings are EOI or talent-pool ads; some roles sit outside Fiji.

Do not model VitiWork on HRmonise. Corporate HCM can remain a later Employer export. The Seeker path is ListingCatalog + Express Interest.
